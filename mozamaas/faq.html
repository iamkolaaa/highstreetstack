Questions & answers

Everything about notice, payment, delivery, refunds and allergies.

Quick answers about pre-ordering from our YO! Sushi kiosks in Accrington and Padiham and our Panku kiosk in Colne.

Ordering and paying

How does ordering work?

Pick your kiosk, add your items, then choose a date and either collection or delivery. You pay by card online, and Stripe emails your receipt. That receipt is your confirmation. We make your order fresh on the day.

How much notice do you need?

24 hours for collection and 48 hours for delivery, counted from the start of your slot. The order page closes each slot 30 minutes before that, to leave time to pay. Orders of more than 15 sharers need 72 hours. Collections from Saturday 19 to Thursday 24 December need 7 days.

How do I pay? Is there a minimum order?

You pay by card online, through Stripe, when you order. We never see your card details. The minimum order is £20 to collect, or £60 for delivery, before any discount.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes. Cancel at least 24 hours before your collection slot or delivery window starts for a full refund to your card. After that we can't refund, because the food is made to order and is perishable. To change or cancel, message the kiosk on [WhatsApp number] or email [email address]. Full terms.

Is there a discount on our first order?

Yes. 10% off your workplace's first pre-order of £40 or more (before the discount, not counting any delivery charge). One per workplace. One code per order. Order by 11:59pm on Friday 18 December 2026. Use the code you were given, such as OFFICE10. Promoter: Moza Maas Ltd, [registered office address]. Full offer terms.

Can I get a VAT receipt or an invoice?

Stripe emails a receipt for every order. If you need a VAT receipt, ask us and we'll send one. Every order is paid by card when you order, so we don't send invoices to pay later.

Collection and delivery

When can I collect?

Between 11:30 and 12:30 at all three kiosks. At Accrington and Padiham you can also collect between 16:00 and 17:00 on Fridays and Saturdays. The order page shows the slots open at each kiosk. Collect from the kiosk counter and show your receipt or give your name. We don't take pre-orders for 25 or 26 December or 1 January.

Do you deliver?

Yes, to workplaces within 5 miles of the kiosk you order from, measured in a straight line. Deliveries run on weekdays only, between 11:30 and 13:00. The order page shows which weekdays each kiosk delivers. You need 48 hours' notice and a minimum order of £60 for delivery. Delivery costs £5, or it's free when your order comes to £80 or more after any discount. There are no deliveries from 19 December 2026 to 3 January 2027. Not sure if you're in range? Choose Deliver on the order page and check your postcode.

Food, allergies and diets

Someone in our team has an allergy. What should I do?

Please talk to the kiosk team before you order, on [WhatsApp number], [phone number] or [email address]. This website doesn't list allergens for each item, but the team can tell you what's in anything. Every item is labelled with full ingredients and allergens. More about allergens.

Is the food halal?

We can't confirm that any of our food is halal, so please don't rely on it being halal. The kiosk team can give you the full ingredients of any item.

Do you have vegan or veggie options?

Yes. We tag vegan and veggie items on the menu as a guide. If you need food that is strictly vegan, or free from egg or milk, check the label or ask us. We also tag items that contain raw fish as a guide. The label on each item lists everything in it.

How should we store and serve the food?

Keep it chilled, below 5 °C, until you serve it. Eat it within 2 hours of taking it out of the fridge, and on the day. Heat-me-up items come chilled: heat until piping hot, once.

Teams and bigger orders

How much should I order for my team?

For a sit-down lunch, order one lunch box each (a set, selection or bento). For sharing, allow about 8–10 pieces of sushi each for lunch, or 5–6 each as part of a buffet. Piece counts are in the item descriptions on the menu where we have them.

Can we set up a regular weekly order?

Yes. Just re-order each week. Nothing is billed automatically, and you can skip any week. Ordering for your workplace.

Can you handle large orders?

Up to a point. Each kiosk can only make a set number of sharers and boxes each day, and the order page tells you if a day is full. More than 15 sharers needs 72 hours' notice. For a really big order, message us before you order so we can check the kiosk can make it on your day.

Can we order for a Christmas party?

Yes. You can book any date the order page shows, up to four weeks ahead. Collections from 19 to 24 December need 7 days' notice, and there are no deliveries from 19 December to 3 January. December Thursdays and Fridays are worth booking early. Christmas orders.
