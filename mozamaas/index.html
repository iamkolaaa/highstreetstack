    Good to know

    Quick answers

    How much notice do you need?

    24 hours to collect, and 48 hours before your requested delivery time for delivery (the order page closes 30 minutes before that). More than 15 sharers needs 72 hours, and collections on 19–24 December need 7 days.

    Can I change or cancel?

    Yes. Email mozamaasltd@gmail.com at least 24 hours before your collection or delivery time for a full refund. After that we've usually started making it.

    Do you show allergens?

    Every box is labelled with full ingredients and allergens. If anyone has an allergy, ask us before you order, by email or at the counter, and add it on the payment page.
