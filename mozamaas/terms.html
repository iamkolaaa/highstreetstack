1. Placing an order

  • Choose your kiosk, your items, a date, and collection or delivery. Then pay by card.
  • You pay by card online, through Stripe, at the time you order. We don't take payment at the counter for pre-orders, and we don't offer credit accounts.
  • Your order is accepted when your payment goes through. The order page then shows your order, and Stripe emails a receipt to the address you gave. Keep the receipt: it's your order confirmation. If it hasn't arrived within an hour, contact us.
  • The minimum order is £20 to collect, or £60 for delivery. Both are counted before any discount.
  • If we can't make something you ordered (for example, an ingredient doesn't arrive, or the store is unexpectedly closed), we'll contact you as soon as we know. We'll offer a swap or a refund for anything we can't make, or a full refund of the whole order if you prefer.

3. Collection

  • Collection is 11:30–12:30 at all three kiosks, every day they take pre-orders. At Accrington and Padiham there is also a 16:00–17:00 slot on Fridays and Saturdays. The order page shows the slots open at each kiosk.
  • We don't take pre-orders for 25 or 26 December or 1 January.
  • Collect from the kiosk counter during your slot. Show your Stripe receipt, or give the name on the order.
  • Running late? Message the kiosk on [WhatsApp number] or phone [phone number]. Food that isn't collected on the day can't be kept, so it can't be refunded.

4. Delivery

  • We deliver to workplaces within 5 miles of the kiosk you order from, measured in a straight line from the kiosk to your postcode. We don't deliver to home addresses.
  • Deliveries run on weekdays only (Monday to Friday). The order page shows which weekdays each kiosk delivers. Deliveries arrive between 11:30 and 13:00. We can't promise an exact time within that window.
  • We don't deliver from Saturday 19 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027.
  • Delivery orders need 48 hours' notice and a minimum order of £60 before any discount.
  • Delivery costs £5. It's free when your order comes to £80 or more, after any discount. The delivery charge is shown in your total before you pay.
  • We hand the food over chilled, in its labelled packaging, at reception or to the person named on the order. Someone needs to be there to take it, because we can't leave chilled food unattended. We don't set up, serve or clear away.
  • If your address turns out to be more than 5 miles from the kiosk, we'll contact you before the day. You can collect instead, or we'll refund you in full.

5. Prices, VAT and receipts

  • Prices are set for each kiosk and shown on the order page. They include VAT where it applies. Most cold sushi is zero-rated. Drinks and some snacks are standard-rated at 20%.
  • The total you pay, including any delivery charge and any discount, is shown before you pay.
  • Stripe emails a receipt for every order. If you need a VAT receipt, ask us and we'll send one.

7. Changes, cancellations and refunds

  • Cancel at least 24 hours before your collection slot or delivery window starts and we'll refund you in full, to the card you paid with. Refunds usually reach your account within 5–10 working days.
  • After that, we can't give a refund. The food is made to order and is perishable, and we'll already be getting it ready.
  • Because the food is perishable, there is no 14-day cooling-off period (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, regulation 28(1)(c)).
  • To change or cancel, message the kiosk on [WhatsApp number] or email [email address]. Give the name on the order and the date. Changes are easiest at least 24 hours before. If a change costs less, we'll refund the difference. If it costs more, we'll tell you how to pay the difference.
  • If we have to cancel your order, we'll refund you in full.

8. If something is wrong

If anything is missing, wrong or not right, please tell us on the same day if you can. A photo helps. We'll put it right with a replacement where we can, or refund the items affected. This doesn't affect your legal rights.

9. Allergens and food safety

  • Every item is labelled with its full ingredients and allergens.
  • This website does not list allergens for each item. If anyone you're ordering for has an allergy, ask the kiosk team before you order: WhatsApp [WhatsApp number], phone [phone number] or email [email address]. See Allergens and food safety.
  • We tag items that contain raw fish on the menu as a guide. The label on each item is the full list of what's in it.
  • Keep the food chilled, below 5 °C, until you serve it. Eat it within 2 hours of taking it out of the fridge, and on the day you collect it or it's delivered.
  • Heat-me-up items come chilled. Heat them until piping hot, and only once.
  • Once we've handed the food over, keeping it chilled and serving it safely is up to you.

Some legal rights apply only to people buying for themselves rather than for a business. The cancellation, refund and "if something is wrong" terms above apply to everyone.
