For workplaces

Team lunches, meeting platters and Friday treats — collected or delivered.

Team lunches and meeting platters from our YO! Sushi kiosks in Accrington and Padiham and our Panku kiosk in Colne. Made fresh on the day. Collect from the counter, or we'll deliver.

Who it's for

  • Offices: team lunches, meetings, training days and leaving dos.
  • Schools: staff room lunches, INSET days and governors' meetings.
  • GP surgeries: practice meetings and a lunch for the team.
  • Care homes: staff lunches, handovers and a thank-you for the team.
  • Car showrooms: Friday team lunches and showroom events.

What you can order

  • Team lunches: one lunch box per person. Choose from the sets, selections and bentos, or let everyone pick their own.
  • Meeting platters: sharers in the middle of the table, with rolls added for variety.
  • Hot options: heat-me-up chicken bites, gyoza, rice bowls and noodles. They come chilled for you to heat.
  • Anything else on the kiosk menu, including sides, desserts and drinks.

Prices are set for each kiosk, shown on the order page, and include VAT where it applies. We tag vegan and veggie items, and items that contain raw fish, on the menu as a guide. The label on each item lists everything in it.

How it works

  1. Choose your nearest kiosk and add your items.
  2. Pick a date, then collection or delivery.
  3. Pay by card online through Stripe. Your receipt is your confirmation.
  4. We make your order fresh on the day.

Collection orders need 24 hours' notice and delivery orders 48 hours. Orders of more than 15 sharers need 72 hours. Collections from 19 to 24 December need 7 days. The minimum order is £20 to collect, or £60 for delivery, before any discount. Each kiosk can only make a set number of sharers and boxes a day, so book busy days early.

Collection

Collect from the kiosk counter between 11:30 and 12:30 at any of the three kiosks. At Accrington and Padiham you can also collect between 16:00 and 17:00 on Fridays and Saturdays. The order page shows the slots open at each kiosk.

  • YO! Sushi, inside Tesco Extra, 2 Eagle Street, Accrington BB5 1LJ
  • YO! Sushi, inside Tesco, Wyre Street, Padiham BB12 8DQ
  • Panku, inside Asda, Swinden Retail Park, Corporation Street, Colne BB8 8LU

Delivery

  • To workplaces within 5 miles of the kiosk you order from, measured in a straight line.
  • Weekdays only, arriving between 11:30 and 13:00. The order page shows which weekdays each kiosk delivers.
  • 48 hours' notice. Minimum order £60 before any discount.
  • £5 delivery, free when your order comes to £80 or more after any discount.
  • No deliveries from 19 December 2026 to 3 January 2027.
  • We bring it chilled, in labelled packaging, to your reception or the person named on the order. Someone needs to be there to take it in. We don't set up, serve or clear away.

Not sure if you're in range? Choose Deliver on the order page and check your postcode.

Weekly orders

If your team likes the same lunch on the same day each week, just re-order each week. It takes a minute. Nothing is billed automatically, there's no standing agreement to sign, and you can skip a week whenever you like.
