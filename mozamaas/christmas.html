Book Thursday and Friday slots early

Work parties often fall on a Thursday or a Friday. Each kiosk can only make so much each morning, and the order page will tell you when a day is full, so please book those days as early as you can.

What to order

  • Sharers for the table. At YO! Sushi (Accrington and Padiham): Luxury Seafood Sushi Sharer, Deluxe Classic Sushi Sharer and Sumptuous Chicken Sharer. At Panku (Colne): Classics Sushi Feast and Salmon Sushi Feast.
  • Rolls and selections to add variety.
  • Heat-me-up bites and gyoza. They come chilled for you to heat.
  • Mochi and dorayaki for afters.

Prices are set for each kiosk, shown on the order page, and include VAT where it applies. We tag items that contain raw fish, and vegan and veggie items, on the menu as a guide. The label on each item lists everything in it.

How much to order

As a buffet, allow about 5–6 pieces of sushi each. As the main lunch, allow about 8–10 pieces each, or order one lunch box per person. Piece counts are in the item descriptions on the menu where we have them.

Collection and delivery

  • Collect between 11:30 and 12:30 at any of the three kiosks. At Accrington and Padiham you can also collect between 16:00 and 17:00 on Fridays and Saturdays.
  • Party in the evening? At Accrington or Padiham, collect in the 16:00–17:00 slot on a Friday or Saturday, keep the food in the fridge, bring it out when you're ready to eat, and eat it that day.
  • We deliver to workplaces within 5 miles of the kiosk (in a straight line), on the weekdays shown on the order page, between 11:30 and 13:00. Minimum order £60 before any discount. £5 delivery, free when your order comes to £80 or more after any discount. No deliveries from 19 December to 3 January.

Keep the party safe

  • Keep the food chilled, below 5 °C, until you serve it.
  • Eat it within 2 hours of taking it out of the fridge, and on the day.
  • Heat-me-up items: heat until piping hot, once.
  • Every item is labelled with full ingredients and allergens. This website doesn't list allergens for each item, so if any guest has an allergy, ask the kiosk team before you order. Allergens and food safety.

Change of plans?

Cancel at least 24 hours before your collection slot or delivery window starts for a full refund. After that we can't refund, because the food is made to order and is perishable. Full terms.
