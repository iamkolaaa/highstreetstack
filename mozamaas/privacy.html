Who we are

Questions about your data? Email us at [email address]. Come to us first. We'll answer, and we'll pass your request to the franchisor where needed. You can also contact the franchisor at [franchisor data protection contact].

Data stored outside the UK

Stripe, Supabase, GitHub and Google are international companies. They may store or access data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, they use safeguards recognised under UK data protection law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you
  • correct anything that's wrong
  • delete your information, unless we have to keep it (for example, tax records)
  • limit how we use it, or object to us using it
  • give you your information in a format you can take elsewhere
  • stop marketing, or withdraw any consent, at any time

To use any of these rights, email [email address]. There's no charge. We'll reply within one month.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first at [email address]. We'll acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and reply as soon as we can.

You can also complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.

Cookies and storage on your device

This website doesn't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. The order page saves your basket, chosen kiosk, collect-or-deliver choice, offer code and delivery postcode in your own browser (local storage), so they're still there if you come back. After you pay, it also keeps a short summary of the order so it can show your confirmation. This stays on your device and isn't used to track you. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. Stripe's payment page uses its own cookies for security and fraud prevention.
