Privacy

Your details, kept simple

Last updated 3 November 2026.

What we keep

When you send a request we keep your name, phone number, the dates or time you picked, and any note or email you add. We also keep the reference number for your request.

Who sees it

Mo, and the person who looks after this website for her. Nobody else. We do not sell your details or use them for adverts.

Where it is stored

In Google Sheets and Google Drive accounts that belong to Mo's business. Google processes it for us on their servers.

Why we keep it

To reply to you, confirm your booking and keep a record of what was agreed. We keep booking records for as long as the business needs them, then delete them.

Your choices

Message Mo on WhatsApp and ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you. She will act on it.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser keeps your request reference for a short time so the confirmation page can show it. Maps come from OpenStreetMap and fonts from Google Fonts, so those services see your device address when a page loads them.
