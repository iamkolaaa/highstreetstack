Nobody Left BehindCommunity fitness, Milton Keynes

A free-to-join fitness community running boxing, track and gym sessions across the week. They needed a site people could register on from Instagram, live attendee counts per session, and paid tickets for two sessions, all without a monthly bill.

The site is static and hosted free. Registration and join forms post to a small backend the organisers can open from their phone. Stripe handles the two paid sessions. Sign-ups now land in one place with a timestamp and a first-timer flag.
