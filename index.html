AI and automation

For the jobs people forget to do

Small businesses lose money on reminders nobody sent, enquiries nobody answered and numbers nobody looked at. I set up the automation that does those jobs, and add AI where it saves someone real time: reading messages, photos and documents, and turning them into something a person can act on.

  • Free-text requests like "tomorrow after work" turned into a real booking slot
  • Weekly sales and staffing reports pulled from till and rota exports, sent to your phone on Monday morning
  • Photos of forms, receipts and paper records read into a spreadsheet
  • Virtual try-on from a customer photo and a product image
  • Enquiries sorted and a reply drafted for you to check and send
