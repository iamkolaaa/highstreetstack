What changes for the garage

📒 Today

  • Customer details in pages of handwritten notes
  • Bookings by phone only, while you're under a car
  • No way to see who's due an MOT
  • Small yard: hard to know what's coming in and where it goes
  • Two sites, two sets of paper

⚡ With this

  • Type the reg, see the customer, their cars and full history
  • Customers book themselves 24/7; one space per site is always kept for walk-ins
  • MOT reminders list with one-tap WhatsApp or text
  • Yard map: what's on the lift, under it, inside and parked outside
  • One system for Z Autoz and Pam's MOT Centre
Running order

The demo in 10 minutes

  1. Open the website on their phone (QR code)."This is what your customers see. Both sites, opening hours, directions, prices."
  2. Use the MOT checker."Put in your MOT date: it tells you the earliest day you can test and keep the same renewal date."
  3. Book a car in on their phone. Use their own reg, pick Tyre fitting or an MOT, choose a time."About a minute, and they get a reference straight away."
  4. Switch to the workshop screen. Within 15 seconds the booking pops up under "Online requests to confirm"."You tap Confirm. Done. No phone call while you're under a car."
  5. Search their reg in the top bar."Every car is matched to its owner by the reg. Next time they're already on file."
  1. Today → Job board. Drag a car from Booked in → On site → In progress → Ready."When it's ready it offers to WhatsApp the customer."
  2. Today → Yard map. Drag cars between the lift, under the lift, inside and the yard."You can see the whole yard at a glance, at both sites."
  3. Open a job. Add a part and labour, record mileage, MOT pass with the new expiry, print the job card.
  4. MOT reminders."Everyone due in the next 30 days. One tap sends the WhatsApp. That's repeat business you're missing today."
  5. Add from paper. Type one customer from their notes."This is how the notebooks get in. Or photograph the pages and we type them up for you."

The demo uses example customers and resets every morning. Anything clicked on the workshop screen stays on that screen and goes back to normal on reload, so nothing can break during the meeting.

To go live

What we need from them

Prices & hours

Real prices for MOT, services, tyres and repairs. Check opening hours and phone numbers for both sites.

The yard

How many cars can be worked on at once at each site, and how many spaces to keep free for walk-ins.

People

Who needs a login, who signs off, and the email or phone that should get new-booking alerts.

The paper records

Photos of the customer notebooks, or a member of staff typing them in with the quick-entry screen.

A web address

Their own domain, e.g. zautoz.co.uk, so the site and booking link are theirs.

Car lookup (optional)

The garage applies for the free DVSA MOT history service so make, model and MOT date fill in from the reg.
