Who we are

When you book at one of our sites, the business at that site is the "controller" of your information under UK GDPR:

  • Pam's MOT Centre Ltd (company no. 09692862), 14 Horwood Court, Bletchley, Milton Keynes MK1 1RD, for bookings at the MOT centre.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • Your vehicle's registration, make, model and MOT date.
  • Your booking and job history, and any notes you or we add.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • Managing your booking and doing the work (contract): so we can book you in, carry out the work and tell you about it.
  • Contacting you about your vehicle (legitimate interests): for example to say the car is ready or that more work is needed.
  • MOT reminders (consent): only if you opt in. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Keeping records (legitimate interests and legal obligation): for warranty, accounting and tax purposes.

Where it's stored and for how long

Your information is stored with our database provider, Supabase, in the EU (London region). We keep it for 6 years after your last visit, for warranty and accounting reasons, then delete it. We don't sell your information.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your information (access);
  • correct anything that's wrong (rectification);
  • delete your information (erasure), where we're not required to keep it;
  • stop using it in certain ways (objection), including stopping MOT reminders.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We'd appreciate the chance to put it right first.

Cookies

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. The site stores a login token in your browser only for staff who sign in. If you're a customer, nothing is stored.
