Questions

Good to know

Can I wait while you work on the car?

Yes for most MOTs, punctures and tyre fits. An MOT takes 45–60 minutes. For longer jobs such as servicing or repairs, tell us when you book and we'll give you a realistic time.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome at both sites. The garage yard is small though, so booking guarantees you a slot and a parking space.

How early can I get my MOT?

Up to one month (minus a day) before it expires, and you keep the same renewal date. Use the checker above to see your earliest date. New cars need their first MOT at 3 years.

What if my car fails its MOT?

If the car stays with us and is retested within 10 working days, the retest is a free partial retest. We'll explain what failed and quote before doing any repair work.

Do you fit part-worn tyres?

Yes. The garage stocks new tyres and quality part-worn ones, fitted and balanced, from brands such as Michelin, Hankook, Pirelli, Continental, Yokohama and Goodyear.

Can I bring my own tyres?

Please call the garage on 01908 365050 first so we can check what's possible.

How do I pay?

Cards and contactless are accepted at both sites.

How do I change or cancel a booking?

Use My booking with your reference and phone number. To change a time, cancel and rebook, or call the site.
