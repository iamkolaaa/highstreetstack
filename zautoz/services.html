MOT facts

What to know about your MOT

  • MOTs are done at Pam's MOT Centre, a DVSA-approved test station for Class 4 (cars) and Class 7 (larger vans) vehicles. A test takes 45–60 minutes.
  • You can test up to one month (minus a day) before expiry and keep the same renewal date.
  • New cars need their first MOT at 3 years.
  • If it fails and stays with us, a partial retest is free if done within 10 working days.
  • The legal maximum fee for a Class 4 MOT is £54.85.
