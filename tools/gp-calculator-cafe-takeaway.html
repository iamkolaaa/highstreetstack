Why GP must use the price without VAT

The VAT in a menu price is not your money. You collect it for HMRC and pass it on, so it cannot count as profit. Gross profit (GP) is worked out on the VAT-exclusive price.

Example. A dish costs £3.00 to make and sells for £10.00 with VAT at 20%.

The same dish, worked two ways
Wrong: use £10.00Right: use £8.33

GP %70.0%64.0%

£10.00 divided by 1.20 is £8.33. Using the full £10.00 makes the dish look 6 points better than it is. If you set a 70% target on the wrong figure, you end up under target on every sale.

Delivery apps take their commission as a share of the price the customer pays. The calculator takes your percentage off the VAT-inclusive price, which is why the GP after commission can be much lower than the GP before it.

Questions people ask

Why do I work out GP on the price without VAT?

The VAT in a menu price is collected for HMRC, not kept by you. Divide the price by 1.20 if the dish is charged VAT at 20%, then take off your ingredient cost. A £10.00 dish costing £3.00 to make has £5.33 GP on £8.33, which is 64.0%, not the 70% you get by using £10.00.

Is takeaway food VAT-free?

Not always. Per gov.uk VAT Notice 709/1, eat-in food and hot takeaway food are standard-rated at 20%. Cold takeaway food is zero-rated, unless it is a type that is always standard-rated, such as crisps, sweets and some drinks.

What GP % should I aim for?

There is no set figure; it depends on your rent, wages and menu. The calculator starts at 70% as a placeholder. Type your own target and it shows the menu price that reaches it.

How does delivery-app commission change my GP?

The calculator takes your commission rate as a share of the VAT-inclusive price the customer pays and subtracts it from your gross profit. Type the rate from your own agreement, as we do not list rates for any app.
