Making Tax Digital for landlords: what changes from April 2027

Who joins on 6 April 2027, how your rental income is counted, how joint property works, and what you send each quarter.

The short version. If your gross rent plus self-employment turnover on your 2025/26 return was over £30,000, you join Making Tax Digital for Income Tax on 6 April 2027. You then send quarterly updates of your property income and costs instead of waiting for one annual return.

What counts as qualifying income

Qualifying income is your gross self-employment turnover plus your gross property income, before expenses. Employment income, dividends, pensions and partnership profit shares do not count. It must be more than the threshold, so exactly £30,000 does not put you in.

HMRC checks the Self Assessment return for the tax year two years before the start date. So the 2025/26 return you file by 31 January 2027 decides whether you start in April 2027. Partnerships are not yet in scope; HMRC will set a timeline later.

Joint property

If you own a property jointly, only your share of the income counts towards the threshold.

Quarterly updates for property income

Once you are in, you send four updates a year. Each is a running total from 6 April: to 5 July (due 7 August), to 5 October (due 7 November), to 5 January (due 7 February) and to 5 April (due 7 May). Calendar quarters have the same due dates. You send totals, not receipts. For solely owned properties, the updates include both income and expenses. For jointly let properties, gov.uk lets you choose to include income and expenses, or income only; if you leave the expenses out, you report them after the end of the tax year. The final declaration replaces the annual return and is due 31 January after the tax year.

If your property turnover is under £90,000, you can record each transaction as just income or expense. Residential landlords still flag finance costs.

What is not changing

Section 24 still works as a tax reduction, not a deduction from rent. The reduction is 20% of the lowest of your finance costs (plus any brought forward), your property profits, or your income above the personal allowance. It cannot create a refund, and unused finance costs carry forward to later years of the same property business. The landlord tax calculator shows what it does to your bill.

Questions people ask

When do landlords have to start Making Tax Digital?

From 6 April 2026 if qualifying income was over £50,000 on the 2024/25 return, from 6 April 2027 if over £30,000 on the 2025/26 return, and from 6 April 2028 if over £20,000 on the 2026/27 return.

What counts as qualifying income for a landlord?

Gross property income plus gross self-employment turnover, before expenses. Employment income, dividends, pensions and partnership profit shares do not count.

How does joint property count?

Only your share counts towards the threshold.

Do I still get the Section 24 tax reduction?

Section 24 works as before: a tax reduction at 20% of the lowest of your finance costs, your property profits, or your income above the personal allowance. Residential landlords still have to flag finance costs.
