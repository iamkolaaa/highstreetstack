Questions people ask

What are payments on account?

Two advance payments towards next year's tax bill, each half of the previous year's Self Assessment bill including Class 4 National Insurance. The first is due 31 January and the second 31 July.

When do I not have to make them?

If your last bill was under £1,000, or if more than 80% of your tax was collected at source, for example through PAYE.

Why is the January bill so large?

On 31 January you pay the balancing payment for the year just ended and the first payment on account for the next. If your income is rising, the balancing payment can be big too.

Can I lower the payments?

If you expect a smaller bill next year, you can ask HMRC to reduce your payments on account. Pay the right amount, because a reduction that turns out too low is charged interest.
