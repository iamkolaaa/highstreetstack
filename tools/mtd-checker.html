Questions people ask

What counts towards the £50,000, £30,000 and £20,000 figures?

Gross self-employment turnover plus gross property income, before any expenses. Pay from a job, dividends, pensions and partnership profit shares do not count. For jointly owned property, only your share counts.

Is it "more than" or "at least"?

More than. If your income is exactly £50,000, you are not over the line. HMRC reads your Self Assessment return from two tax years before the start, so the 2024/25 return decides April 2026, 2025/26 decides April 2027 and 2026/27 decides April 2028.

What happens if I miss a quarterly update?

Each missed deadline earns a penalty point. At four points you pay £200, and £200 for each further miss. There are no points for late quarterly updates in 2026/27, but a late return still earns points.

Can I be exempt?

You can apply to HMRC if it is not reasonable for you to use software, for example because of age, health, disability, religious belief or having no internet. Not being used to software is not accepted. Partnerships are not in scope yet.
