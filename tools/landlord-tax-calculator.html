Questions people ask

What is Section 24?

Since 6 April 2020, landlords of residential property cannot deduct mortgage interest from their rent before working out tax. Instead you take a tax reduction of 20% of your finance costs.

Who does it cost money?

Mostly higher-rate taxpayers, and basic-rate taxpayers whose rental profit pushes them into the higher rate. If you pay basic rate either way, the 20% reduction matches the 20% tax you would have saved, and the cost is nil.

How do joint owners work it out?

Each owner takes their own share of the rent, costs and interest, and is taxed on that share with their own other income. Type your ownership share and use the full property figures.

Can I use the £1,000 property allowance as well?

Not if you claim the finance cost reduction on residential property. You also cannot use it with Rent a Room. If you take the allowance, you cannot deduct actual expenses.
