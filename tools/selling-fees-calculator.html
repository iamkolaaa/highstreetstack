Questions people ask

Does Vinted charge sellers a fee?

Vinted's seller help says there are no fees for selling. The buyer pays a protection fee on top of the price, so it does not come off your profit. Extras such as bumps cost more.

Do eBay private sellers pay fees?

UK private sellers pay no final value fee (not motors). eBay gives 300 free listings a month, then charges 35p each. Business sellers pay a final value fee, a per-order fee and a regulatory fee.

What does Etsy charge a seller?

A 6.5% transaction fee on the order total including postage, payment processing of 4% + 20p for a UK bank account, and a 0.48% regulatory operating fee. The listing fee is 0.20 USD, which Etsy converts to pounds.

Does Depop charge a selling fee?

Depop says there are no selling fees for UK sellers. Payment processing is 2.9% + 30p on the item price plus shipping and any taxes. Boosted listings cost extra.
