Questions people ask

Why is the percentage not a round 20%?

The first £12,570 of income is tax-free, Class 4 National Insurance is 6% above the same figure, and higher rate tax starts above £50,270. The share you set aside goes up as profit goes up.

Do I have to pay Class 2 National Insurance?

Not since 6 April 2024. Profits at or above £7,105 are treated as if you had paid it. Below that you can pay voluntarily, at £3.65 a week, to build up State Pension record. Tick the box to see the cost.

What counts as profit?

Your sales less costs you can claim, such as materials, mileage and insurance. If your sales are under the £1,000 trading allowance, you generally do not need to report them, and above it you can take the allowance instead of your costs.

Does this cover payments on account?

No. This shows the tax on one year's profit. Payments on account are a separate cash-flow question about when you pay, and the payments on account calculator covers it.
