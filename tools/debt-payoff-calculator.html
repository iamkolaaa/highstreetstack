How the sums work
  1. Each month, interest is added to every balance: balance × APR ÷ 12.
  2. Every debt gets its minimum payment.
  3. Whatever is left of your monthly budget goes to one debt. Avalanche picks the highest APR. Snowball picks the smallest balance.
  4. When a debt is cleared, its minimum rolls into the pool, so the same total keeps going to the next one.

Which method to pick

Avalanche always costs the least interest, because the dearest debt goes first. Snowball clears small debts sooner, so you tick things off earlier. If the two come out within a few pounds of each other, take the one you will stick with.

The figures treat the APR as a flat yearly rate charged monthly and assume you add nothing new to the cards. Real cards recalculate daily and have promotional rates, so use this to compare methods, not to predict the last penny. If a minimum payment is lower than the interest charged, that debt never shrinks on its own and the tool says so.
