Questions people ask

How do I work out what to charge for a clean?

Time the job, multiply the hours by your hourly rate, then add the cost of products and travel. If you price by area, multiply the square metres by your price per m² and still count your hours, so you can see what you earn per hour.

What is an equivalent hourly rate?

The price minus travel and products, divided by the hours the job takes. It shows what each hour actually earns you, whichever way you priced the job.

How do I price an end of tenancy clean?

Add up the time: so many minutes per bedroom, per bathroom and a fixed base for the rest of the property, plus an uplift for the deep clean. Then multiply by your hourly rate and add products and travel. The calculator starts with 60 minutes a bedroom, 75 a bathroom and 150 minutes base.

How is a regular client's monthly value worked out?

The price per visit, less any discount you give, times the number of visits in a month: 52 a year divided by 12 for weekly, 26 for fortnightly, 13 for every four weeks.
