How it works

BUY

Tell Babs your budget and the car you want. He sends you the auction listings, the photos and a condition report, and bids only on the lot you approve.

SHIP

RoRo for most cars, a container when you want extra protection or to send belongings with it. USA to Lagos takes three to five weeks at sea.

CLEAR

Customs paperwork and duty are handled at Tin Can or Apapa before the car leaves the port.

DELIVER

The car is driven or trucked to your address. You get photos at every step: bought, loaded, sailed, landed, delivered.

Babs drives it himself

Babs Auto Logistics is run by Babs himself. He buys at Copart and IAAI, loads the containers and drives the transporter. Nothing is handed to a middleman.

He also fixes and sells cars in the UK. If you are in the UK and want a car shipped to family in Nigeria, he does that too.

Questions people ask

How long does shipping take? USA to Lagos is three to five weeks at sea. From the UK it is two to four weeks. Your quote gives an arrival window before you pay anything.

RoRo or container? RoRo means the car is driven onto the ship; it is cheaper and fine for most cars. A container costs more, protects the car fully, and you can ship personal items inside it.

Can you buy a specific car for me? Yes. Give a budget and what you want. For common models Babs usually has options within three to seven working days; rare cars can take up to three weeks.

Do I pay before you bid? No. You approve the exact lot and price first. You see the photos and condition report before any bid is placed.

What if the car is not as described? Every car is checked and photographed before purchase. If it differs from the listing, Babs takes it up with the auction house, including cancelling or adjusting the price.

What does the price on the site include? The car, shipping to Lagos and port handling. Duty and clearing are quoted separately.

Can I ship a car I already own? Yes, from the US or the UK.

How do I pay? By bank transfer in naira, pounds or dollars, in stages: a deposit, then on purchase, then on arrival.

Can I come and see the UK cars? Yes, by appointment.

Where do you deliver in Nigeria? Anywhere. Delivery in Lagos is included; other states are quoted.
