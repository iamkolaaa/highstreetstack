From a US auction to your gate

Three stages, five to seven weeks door to door. You get photos at every one: on the lot, on the truck, in the container, at the port and at your gate.

Buy

Send Babs the car you want and your budget. He finds it at Copart, IAAI or a dealer, sends you the photos and the condition report, and bids only on the lot you approve. His own transporter collects it.

Load

Most cars go RoRo, driven straight onto the ship. Choose a container when you want the car fully covered or have belongings to send with it. Cars are racked and strapped before the doors close.

Land

Three to five weeks later it lands at Tin Can or Apapa. Babs clears it at the port and has it driven to your gate, or you collect it from the Lagos yard.

Questions people ask

  • Shipping from the US takes three to five weeks.
  • You pay in stages, never before you approve the car.
  • Cars in Lagos can be viewed by appointment.
How long does shipping take?

From the US to Lagos it is three to five weeks at sea. From the UK it is two to four weeks. Your quote gives you an arrival window before you pay anything.

RoRo or container?

RoRo is cheaper and right for most cars: the car is driven onto the ship. A container costs more, keeps the car fully covered and lets you ship personal things inside it.

Do I pay before you bid?

No. You approve the exact car and the most you want to pay first. You see the photos and the condition report before any bid goes in.

Can you find a specific car for me?

Yes. Tell Babs the make, model, year and budget. Common models usually take three to seven working days to find; rarer ones can take up to three weeks.

What if the car is not as described?

Every car is checked and photographed before it is bought. If it differs from the listing, Babs takes it up with the auction house, including cancelling the sale or getting the price cut.

What do the prices on this site include?

For cars in Lagos and on the water: the car, shipping and clearing at the port. Delivery outside Lagos is quoted separately. UK cars are priced as they stand in the UK.
