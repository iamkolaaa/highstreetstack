Privacy

This site does not use cookies, tracking or analytics, and it does not collect anything you type.

When you message us

If you contact Babs Auto Logistics on WhatsApp, by email or by phone, we use your name, number and the details of the car you want to answer you, quote, buy, ship and deliver the car. We keep those messages for as long as we are dealing with your car and for six years after for our accounts.

Who sees it

Only the people who handle your car: our team, the auction house, the shipping line and the clearing agent at the port. We never sell your details.

Your rights

Ask us at any time for a copy of what we hold about you, or for it to be deleted, at info@babsautologistics.com.
