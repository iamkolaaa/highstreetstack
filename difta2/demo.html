What changes for the studio.

Today, on Booksy

  • Monthly fees for a platform that isn't yours
  • Your 465 five-star reviews live on Booksy's page, not yours
  • Clients see other barbers' adverts while they book
  • No loyalty scheme, no easy way to win back regulars
  • No website of your own to send people to

With this

  • Your own site in your brand: black marble, the logo, your work
  • Clients book online or just text “BOOK” on WhatsApp
  • Payment up front by card or Apple Pay, straight to your bank
  • Every client saved automatically, with notes, visits and spend
  • “Due a cut” list and one-tap WhatsApp rebook messages
  • The Art Club: every 10th cut free, tracked for you
Running order

The demo in 10 minutes.

  1. Hand him your phone on the website (QR code).This is what your clients see. Your studio, your team, your work.
  2. Tap a story circle, then swipe the team cards.Your Instagram, built into your own site.
  3. Show the live badge at the top.It shows the next free chair, straight from your diary.
  4. Book a cut with his own name. Pick a barber, service and time, then pay with the demo card.Thirty seconds, paid up front. No more no-shows.
  5. Show the confirmation. Confetti, loyalty stamp and the WhatsApp message they'd get.They get their account automatically. No password, no app.
  1. Tap the green WhatsApp button and book by chat.Or they just text BOOK. It books them in at 2am while you're asleep.
