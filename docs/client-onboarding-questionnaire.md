# Client onboarding questionnaire

Send before the first proper meeting. Takes the client 15 to 20 minutes. Their answers become the build brief, the sitemap and the CRM setup.

How to use: copy the core questions plus the add-on block for their trade into WhatsApp or email. Don't send more than 25 questions. Skip anything you already know.

---

## Core questions, every client

### 1. Your business in one breath
1. What does your business do, in one or two sentences, as you'd say it to a stranger?
2. Who are your best customers? Age, area, what they come to you for.
3. What do customers say they like about you? Any reviews or messages you're proud of, send them.
4. Who are your two or three nearest competitors, and what do you do better?

### 2. What you sell
5. List your services or products with prices. A photo of your price board is fine.
6. Which three services make you the most money, or do you most want more of?
7. Anything you want to stop doing or don't want advertised?

### 3. How customers reach you today
8. How do people find you now? Tick all: word of mouth, Google Maps, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Booksy or similar, walk past, flyers, other.
9. How do they book or ask questions? WhatsApp, phone, Instagram DMs, an app, walk in?
10. What goes wrong with that? Missed messages, no-shows, double bookings, chasing deposits, forgetting to follow up?
11. Roughly how many enquiries and bookings a week?

### 4. The website
12. Do you have a website or domain name already? If yes, the address and where it's registered.
13. What must the site do on day one? Tick: show services and prices, take bookings, take enquiries, show opening hours and map, show reviews, sell gift vouchers, show a gallery, take payments, other.
14. Send two or three websites you like, from any trade, and say what you like about each.
15. Do you have a logo, brand colours, or fonts? Send files if so. If not, say the feel you want in three words, for example "clean, local, trusted".
16. Photos: do you have good photos of the place, the team and the work? If not, are you happy for me to take some on a visit?

### 5. Facts I need for the site
17. Full address, phone number, email you want shown, opening hours including holidays.
18. Links to every social account you use.
19. Do you have a Google Business Profile? If yes, who has the login?
20. Names, roles and a line about each team member you want shown, if any.

### 6. Customer data and messages
21. Who will check enquiries day to day, and on what device?
22. Should customers get an automatic reply, a booking confirmation, or a reminder the day before? Which of those?
23. Do you keep customer details anywhere now: a diary, a spreadsheet, an app, your phone? Would you want that moved across?
24. Anyone else who needs access, like a partner or receptionist?

### 7. Timing and money
25. Any date this needs to be live by, like a reopening or event?
26. Rough budget for setup, and whether a small monthly fee for hosting, updates and the CRM is workable.

## Add-on blocks, pick the one that fits

### Garage (Z Autoz Ltd)
- Which jobs do you want online booking for: MOT, service, diagnostics, tyres, repairs, other?
- Do you need the customer's registration number at booking? Do you use a DVLA lookup or MOT-history tool today?
- Courtesy car or collection offered? Payment on collection or upfront deposit?
- Do you want MOT-due reminders sent to past customers a month before? That's the strongest repeat-business tool we can build.
- Any trade associations, warranties or certifications to show, like RMI, IMI or manufacturer approvals?

### Barbers (Do It For The Art Studio)
- You use Booksy today. Keep it and link to it, or move bookings onto your own site? Booksy charges per booking; your own costs nothing but needs setting up.
- Walk-ins allowed, or appointment only?
- Each barber's name, specialities and Instagram, if you want individual booking.
- Price list per service and per barber if it varies.
- No-show or late cancellation policy, and whether you take deposits.

### Fitness community (Find More Fitness)
- Weekly timetable: session, day, time, location, price or free, capacity.
- How do people join today, and do you want a sign-up form with waiver or consent text?
- Paid sessions: how are they paid now, and should the site take payment?
- Do you want attendee counts shown live on the site, like NLB?
- Instagram is your main channel: should the site pull your latest posts, or just link?

## After they reply

1. Paste answers into `clients/<name>/brief.md` in the client repo.
2. Turn question 13 into the sitemap and question 22 into the automation list.
3. Question 10 is the sales pitch for the CRM. Quote it back to them in the proposal.
4. Book a 30-minute call to fill any blanks and take photos.
